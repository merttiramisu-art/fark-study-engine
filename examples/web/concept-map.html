    What FARK can—and cannot—say

    All observations are tied to current question revisions.

    The content links are editorial declarations, not discovered causal relations. The colored evidence markers represent answer-key observations only. No persistent profile, mastery probability, diagnosis or teaching-effectiveness claim is produced.

    The built-in example uses original fictional arithmetic content. Author-pasted content is not independently fact-checked or license-verified. All processing stays in browser memory; refresh or reset clears answers. No accounts, remote assets, paid services or analytics.
