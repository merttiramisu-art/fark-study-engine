Which fraction is larger?

LINKED MINI LESSONORIGINAL EXAMPLE

Same numerator, different-sized pieces

A whole cut into 3 equal parts has larger pieces than the same whole cut into 4 equal parts. So 1/3 is larger than 1/4. Compare the size of a single piece, not just the denominator.
